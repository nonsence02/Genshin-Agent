# Golden Frostbound Oath

## Характеристики
- Тип: Bow
- Редкость: 5★
- Базовая атака: 44 - 542
- Вторичная характеристика: CRIT DMG
- Пассивный навык: Increases DEF by. When the equipping character's Elemental Skill or Lunar-Crystallize attack(s) hits enemies, gain the Frost Fae's Favor for: Geo DMG inflicted by the equipping character increases by, Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG increases by. While this is active, if there are Moondrifts near the equipping character, all other nearby party members Will gain the Frost Fae's Mischief: Geo DMG dealt increases by and Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG increases by. This can be triggered even when the equipping character is off-field.

## Материалы для прокачки
- Материалы: Ember of Long Night Flint, Faded Flaming Hilt, Tattered Warrant, Afterglow of Long Night Flint, Fractured Flaming Hilt, Immaculate Warrant, Flare of Long Night Flint, Jeweled Flaming Hilt, Frost-Etched Warrant, Blaze of Long Night Flint

## Стандартная стоимость прокачки (Справочно)
- **Возвышение (до 90 ур.):** 5 зеленых, 14 синих, 14 фиолетовых, 6 золотых материалов (из подземелий); 15/18/27 с элитных врагов; 10/15/18 с обычных врагов.
