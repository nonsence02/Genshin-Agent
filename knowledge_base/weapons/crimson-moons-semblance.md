# Crimson Moons Semblance (Crimson Moon's Semblance)

## Характеристики
- Тип: Polearm
- Редкость: 5★
- Базовая атака: 48 - 674
- Вторичная характеристика: CRIT Rate
- Пассивный навык: Grants a Bond of Life equal to of Max HP when a Charged Attack hits an opponent. This can be triggered up to once every. In addition, when the equipping character has a Bond of Life, they gain a DMG Bonus; if the value of the Bond of Life is greater than or equal to of Max HP, then gain an additional DMG.
