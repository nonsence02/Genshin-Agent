# Prospectors Drill (Prospector's Drill)

## Характеристики
- Тип: Polearm
- Редкость: 4★
- Базовая атака: 44 - 565
- Вторичная характеристика: ATK
- Пассивный навык: When the wielder is healed or heals others, they will gain a Unity's Symbol that lasts, up to a maximum of Symbols. When using their Elemental Skill or Burst, all Symbols will be consumed and the Struggle will be granted for. For each Symbol consumed, gain ATK and All Elemental DMG Bonus. The Struggle can be triggered once every, and Symbols can be gained even when the character is not on the field.

## Материалы для прокачки
- Материалы: Fragment of an Ancient Chord, Old Operative's Pocket Watch, Meshing Gear, Chapter of an Ancient Chord, Operative's Standard Pocket Watch, Mechanical Spur Gear, Movement of an Ancient Chord, Operative's Constancy, Artificed Dynamic Gear, Echo of an Ancient Chord

## Стандартная стоимость прокачки (Справочно)
- **Возвышение (до 90 ур.):** 5 зеленых, 14 синих, 14 фиолетовых, 6 золотых материалов (из подземелий); 15/18/27 с элитных врагов; 10/15/18 с обычных врагов.
