# Tome of the Eternal Flow

## Характеристики
- Тип: Catalyst
- Редкость: 5★
- Базовая атака: 44 - 542
- Вторичная характеристика: CRIT DMG
- Пассивный навык: HP is increased by. When current HP increases or decreases, Charged Attack DMG will be increased by for. Max stacks, can be triggered once every. When you have stacks or refresh a third stack's duration, Energy will be restored. This Energy restoration can be triggered once every.

## Материалы для прокачки
