# Athame Artis

## Характеристики
- Тип: Sword
- Редкость: 5★
- Базовая атака: 46 - 608
- Вторичная характеристика: CRIT Rate
- Пассивный навык: CRIT DMG from Elemental Bursts is increased by. When an Elemental Burst hits an opponent, gain the Blade of the Daylight Hours: ATK is increased by. Nearby active party members other than the equipping character have their ATK increased by for. Additionally, when the party possesses Hexerei: Secret Rite effects, the effects of Blade of the Daylight Hours are increased by an additional. This can be triggered even if the equipping character is off-field.
