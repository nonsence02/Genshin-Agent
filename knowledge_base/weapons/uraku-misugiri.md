# Uraku Misugiri

## Характеристики
- Тип: Sword
- Редкость: 5★
- Базовая атака: 44 - 542
- Вторичная характеристика: CRIT DMG
- Пассивный навык: Normal Attack DMG is increased by and Elemental Skill DMG is increased by. After a nearby active character deals Geo DMG, the aforementioned effects increase by for. Additionally, the wielder's DEF is increased by.
